# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

EV Toolkit helps you estimate electric vehicle and fuel vehicle costs, charging costs and driving range, and manage vehicle profiles. This policy explains how EV Toolkit handles information in the app and when you contact support.

## Information stored on your device

EV Toolkit stores settings and saved vehicle profiles locally. These include vehicle names and parameters you enter, calculator defaults, currency, units and appearance preferences. Calculator session values are held in memory while the app is running; closing a session is not the same as deleting saved defaults or vehicle profiles.

EV Toolkit does not automatically send these values to an EV Toolkit server. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account-based tracking or its own cloud synchronization service. It currently has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

If saved settings or vehicle records are damaged, the app may preserve local recovery copies before replacing the damaged data. These copies may contain earlier vehicle names, parameters or settings. They remain on the device and are not automatically sent to support.

## Retention and deletion of local data

Saved settings and vehicle profiles remain in the app's local storage until changed, deleted or removed with the app's data. Deleting a vehicle removes its active profile, but an earlier version may remain in a recovery copy. Recovery copies do not currently expire automatically and there is no separate in-app control to delete them.

To remove all local EV Toolkit data, including recovery copies:

- On Android, use the device's app settings to clear EV Toolkit's storage/data, or uninstall the app. Clearing only the cache does not remove all saved data.
- On iOS, choose **Delete App**. **Offload App** preserves app documents and data and does not perform this deletion.

EV Toolkit support cannot remotely access or erase data stored only on your device.

Your operating system or device backup service may include app data in device backups or restore it later, depending on your settings. Removing local app data does not necessarily remove existing device or cloud backups. Manage those copies through your device or backup provider. EV Toolkit does not operate that backup service.

## Optional feedback by email

Choosing **Send feedback** prepares a message in your email app. The app does not send the message automatically. You can review, edit or discard it before sending.

The prepared message may include:

- app name, version and build number;
- operating system and version;
- device model and model identifier, such as iPhone17,2.

A model identifier describes a device model, not a unique individual device. EV Toolkit does not include an advertising identifier, serial number or unique device identifier in the prepared message. Saved vehicle profiles and calculator values are not automatically attached. Any information or attachments you add yourself will also be included if you send them.

If you send the message, EV Toolkit support receives your email address and the message contents. We use them only to respond to your request and diagnose the issue you report. Sending feedback is optional and is not required to use the calculators or saved vehicles.

## Retention and deletion of support emails

We keep support correspondence while handling your request and for no longer than **three months after the request is resolved**, then delete it from our support mailbox. This is a support mailbox practice; the app does not automatically manage or delete emails.

You can request earlier deletion of your support correspondence by emailing **ev.toolkit.app@gmail.com**, preferably from the address used for the original request. Identify the conversation you want removed; do not send unnecessary identity documents or sensitive information. We may need to confirm that the request concerns your correspondence before deleting it.

Deleting correspondence from our mailbox does not delete copies in your own email account. Your email provider and our email provider process email under their own policies, including their handling of backups and deleted messages.

## Sharing and external services

EV Toolkit does not sell your information or automatically share saved vehicle profiles and calculator values with third parties. Support email is handled through email providers for delivery and storage. Information you choose to send to support is used for the support purposes described above.

If you open an external legal or support page, your browser contacts that website and its hosting provider under their own policies. These pages are currently hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening an external page does not automatically transmit your saved vehicle profiles or calculator values to it.

## Children

EV Toolkit does not require age or identity information and is not designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so that we can address the request and remove the correspondence.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when information practices change. The current policy is available through the app's Privacy Policy link and the app store listing. The date above identifies the latest revision.

## Contact

For support, privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, contact **ev.toolkit.app@gmail.com**. You can also use **Send feedback** in the app.
